## 4. Decision, unresolved owner choices and preserved state

**Technical decision: Accepted for Owner Change-Control Decision — Design Only.** v1.1 resolves the v1.0 review's missing *proposal contracts* for R2, R5, R6 and R7, strengthens the R3/R4/R9 requirements, and preserves R1/R8. The requirements are coherent and observable enough to put to an owner. They are **not** a concrete, owner-approved source-change mechanism. A source-modification authorization cannot be derived from this technical review while the following choices remain open:

1. Independent trust root, trusted outer entry, atomic durable replay-resistant attempt ledger and independently retained claim/failure record.
2. Independent creation/assignment receipt producer and integrity anchor, one-use role/ARM issuer, and complete Node/conhost/helper membership method.
3. Qualifying bounded, non-destructive worker and identity-helper exit/isolation boundary, including whether the proposed `≤5,000 ms` helper ceiling is approved or replaced.
4. Independent evidence-integrity/failure sink, destination, schema and retention authority, including post-terminal audit failure.
5. Independently approved manifest/environment/executable identity chain and any separate dependency, service, key, privilege, configuration or architecture change control it entails.

Owner review must make or explicitly defer those decisions before a concrete source-change contract can be authorized. Any choice that affects the approved platform/Gateway, tenant, authorization, audit, or implementation/release boundaries must follow the baseline architecture-change procedure. The next governed sequence is owner/change-control decision → separately authorized revised exact source/configuration → exact-source/path preflight → source safety review → separate R&D execution authorization → at most one authorized R&D attempt → immutable direct evidence and independent review. A proposal review cannot skip any stage.

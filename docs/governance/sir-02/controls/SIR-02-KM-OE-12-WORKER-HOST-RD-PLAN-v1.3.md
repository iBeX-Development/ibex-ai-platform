## 3. Proposed eligibility and current STOP

The only **proposed eligible force action** is bounded controlled termination by an **independently authorized outer owner** of a **fully verified, exclusive, single-run-owned boundary**, after an approved timeout and cooperative grace. Before action, independently sealed creation/admission evidence must account for every actual Node, Worker, helper, conhost where present and host-managed member; bind the run/generation, handle/session/Job and boundary; and support fresh full-identity revalidation immediately before the call. The action must affect no unidentified or unrelated process/resource. The exact reviewed path/API, measured start/end, native result, external failure evidence and independently observed final member/boundary/handle/session state are mandatory. Unknown ownership, incomplete admission, unavailable evidence, failed native/observer result or missed bound means **`STOP — Disposition Unproven`**. No fallback may bypass that STOP.

**Current effective rule:** controlled termination remains prohibited under the existing strict Worker-Host R&D and bounded-worker direction. This draft does not approve a mechanism, prove finite disposal, close GAP-A or GAP-B, reverse the rejected technical preflight, or authorize source/environment changes or R&D execution. The Option B design STOP and worker-disposal proof obligation remain open. `Controlled termination = NOT APPROVED`; `Technical Preflight = REJECTED`; `R&D Execution = NOT AUTHORIZED`; `Attempt = NOT STARTED`; `OE-12 = Not Executed`; `SIR-02 = Blocking`.

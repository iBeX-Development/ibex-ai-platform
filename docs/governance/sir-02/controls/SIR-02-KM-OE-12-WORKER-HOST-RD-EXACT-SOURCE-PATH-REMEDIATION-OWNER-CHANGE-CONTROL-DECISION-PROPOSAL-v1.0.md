## 1. Decision scope and controlling baseline

This is a **decision proposal**, not an owner decision or a concrete source-change authorization. It presents mutually distinguishable candidate control choices for the five unresolved items in the accepted v1.1 design review. Each candidate is conditional; the owner may explicitly defer any item. An item without a selected, compatible and separately approved mechanism remains **Unresolved** and blocks source-change authorization for that item. No combination is asserted feasible by this proposal.

The active constraints include `docs/baseline/README.md` (approved v1.0 package and architecture-change procedure), `docs/baseline/02-tenant-security-contract-v1.0.md`, `04-ai-gateway-contract-v1.0.md`, `08-audit-observability-architecture-v1.0.md`, `16-technical-design-v1.0.md`, and `17-implementation-plan-v1.0.md` (especially the standalone platform/Gateway boundary and staged security/evidence gates). The scoped OE-12 criterion, Acceptance Matrix and Execution Plan overlays and audit addendum became effective only through `SIR-02-KM-OE-12-BASELINE-ACTIVATION-OWNER-DECISION-v1.0.md` at `da524cb006253fb0b6a2df79651b830febc5acdd`. `SIR-02-KM-OE-12-ALIGNMENT-GAP-CLOSURE-DECISION-v1.0.md` at `87f98ece53ecd0773137722cc184404743c541f8` closes governance consistency only. The active GAP-B criterion is **Authoritative Validation-Boundary Quiescence**, which does not claim physical CIM-provider quiescence. GAP-A and bounded worker disposal remain direct-evidence obligations. Any material platform, Gateway, tenant, authorization, audit or release-boundary change follows the baseline architecture-change procedure and separate change control.

The prepared package governance manifest is `SIR-02-KM-OE-12-WORKER-HOST-RD-SOURCE-CONFIG-MANIFEST-v1.0.md` (SHA-256 `a1a8afe78fd9f4f0499c5456787ff6ef24a150338b594cb28ac4d466e1e8b9f2`); its machine manifest is `tests/rd/oe12-worker-host/package-manifest.json` (SHA-256 `03d863808a642768151b0b675618bc99fa1e3108304e4f857d92dc6c3c597a33`). Matching committed payload hashes prove the *prepared* bytes, not a trustworthy future authorization, runnable copy, executable selection or safe runtime behavior. The rejected preflight's self-supplied authorization/hash, direct entry, incomplete member set, helper lifetime, partial-result and audit-failure findings remain controlling. The v1.1 review accepts a reviewable contract, not a repaired package.

**Cross-cutting acceptance rule:** each later source path must map `trigger → exact reviewed file/branch → exact API/call → state transition → ownership check → cleanup/disposal → retained evidence`. Before any process/resource-affecting action, revalidate `PID + image path + parent PID + creation time + run token + creation/assignment evidence` and applicable handle/session/Job and complete member facts. An unknown member, stale or mismatched identity, missing field or PID reuse stops the action; exception and fallback cannot bypass it. No unknown-member Job-wide termination, PID-only termination or destructive fallback is authorized. Exactly one immutable `Success | Timeout | Fail | Cancelled` terminal state and Accepted Result Cutoff remain separate from candidate evidence eligibility and worker lifecycle. **GAP Closure = Approved Applicable Design/Boundary AND Newly Authorized Direct Evidence.**

## 2. Owner choice T — independent authority and one-use attempt ledger

Current `tests/rd/oe12-worker-host/guard.mjs:executionGate()` accepts caller-supplied authorization and hashes; `controller.mjs:main()` can create audit material and spawn after that gate, and `supervisor.mjs`, `worker.ps1` and `identity.ps1` have separately callable entries. Static imports occur before `main()`; any side-effecting import must be accounted for. The future trusted outer entry must atomically and durably claim **one** run/generation/case/package/evidence destination before runnable-source copy, candidate import, audit creation, worker/helper creation or other execution-relevant side effect. Read-only preclaim verification is permitted. A failed claim causes zero candidate influence; a crash after claim leaves it consumed, never retryable under the same authority.

An HMAC made by the Controller over a received receipt does not establish the receipt producer's independent provenance. Membership alone is not ownership.

## 4. Owner choice D — bounded non-destructive disposal and helper ceiling

The current `supervisor.mjs:observeIdentity()` and `controller.mjs:independentIdentity()` can time out, unref a helper and destroy pipes without observing helper exit. `WH-03` and `WH-09` STOP before worker spawn; `WH-08` is a synthetic unexpected-member STOP. None proves real hung-worker disposal or complete membership. `T_dispose = 5,000 ms` and `T_total = 45,000 ms` remain separate approved-plan proposals; v1.1 adds **`T_helper_end_to_end ≤ 5,000 ms` as a proposed, unapproved ceiling** measured before helper spawn to observed exit/all handles closed or a separately approved qualifying safe isolation state. The helper deadline cannot reset in catch/finally or extend startup, total-run or active-disposal deadlines.

A helper deadline is the minimum of its own approved ceiling, remaining total-run budget and remaining active-disposal budget. No destructive Job-wide or unknown-PID fallback is a candidate.

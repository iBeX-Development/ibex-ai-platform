## 2. Recommended interpretation for a future owner decision — not approved

1. A **fully verified, exclusive, single-run-owned isolation boundary may be eligible for bounded termination after an authorized timeout**, only if an independent owner proves its authority, every admitted member and any outside-boundary effect before action, performs fresh full-identity revalidation immediately before action, and observes the post-action state within the approved deadline. The full member evidence must include actual Node, Worker, identity-helper and conhost processes where present, host-managed members, creation/assignment events, run/generation, retained handles/sessions/Job facts where applicable, and `PID + image path + parent PID + creation time + run token`. An unknown, stale, missing, reused or unexpected identity fails closed. Termination of an unidentified or unrelated process remains prohibited. This proposed action is **controlled termination**, not “non-destructive termination”; no current record authorizes it.
2. A **paused-but-live** VM or compute system may count only as **temporary isolation** if independent evidence proves the authoritative result is fenced and no external influence continues. It does **not** satisfy Worker/helper disposal, observed process exit, resource/handle/session closure, or finite completion. A pause cannot silently satisfy the five-second disposal/safe-isolation acceptance gate; the final disposition deadline and evidence must be separately specified and approved.
3. If exclusivity, complete admission, action authority, outside-boundary protection, a non-resetting finite deadline, or post-action state cannot be independently proved, the proposed action **fails closed** and **D2 remains `Design STOP`**. No Job-wide, VM-wide, compute-system-wide, PID, handle-close, watchdog, timeout, exception, or `finally` fallback may enlarge authority or affect an unidentified/unrelated process. An unresolved resource is recorded as failure, never as disposal proof.

The owner must decide whether this recommended meaning is acceptable. Until a separate versioned amendment is reviewed, approved and effected, “bounded non-destructive worker disposal” remains an open obligation under the currently effective text; this proposal must not be treated as permission to force a VM/container shutdown. The physical provider may continue work after authoritative closure, but late activity/results must still be unable to mutate the terminal OE-12 outcome.

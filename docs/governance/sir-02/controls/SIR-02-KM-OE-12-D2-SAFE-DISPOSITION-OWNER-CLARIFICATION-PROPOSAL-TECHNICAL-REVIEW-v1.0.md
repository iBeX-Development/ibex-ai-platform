## 2. Clause-by-clause impact

The **proposed interpretation** in the table means only this conditional idea: after an authorized timeout, controlled termination of an independently verified, exclusive, single-run-owned boundary *might* become eligible if all admitted members and outside effects are independently accounted for, the complete identity is freshly revalidated immediately before action, and finite post-action disposition is observed. Unknown/unrelated-process effects remain prohibited. Pause is temporary isolation, never disposal. No current clause is silently reinterpreted as granting that action.

## 3. Two owner decision paths

| Path | Governance result | Safety and technical consequence |

| **Preserve current criterion** | No controlled termination is approved or introduced. Keep existing active OE-12 criterion, matrix/plan overlays and Worker-Host cleanup contract. | D2 **`Design STOP` remains**. The second path does **not** relax T1 independent verifier and atomic single-use attempt claim, P1 independent receipt/complete membership, E1 protected failure evidence, M1 complete manifest/byte identity, GAP-A fresh revalidation, immutable terminal state/cutoff, zero post-closure authoritative mutation, or the no-unknown-process rule. It strengthens the stated pause distinction. It has no operational proof: exact Node/conhost/helper membership, real Worker/helper hangs, outside-boundary protection, observed exits/disposal and numeric deadlines still need **newly authorized direct R&D evidence** after a separately accepted exact-source preflight. A VM or Hyper-V-isolated container shutdown/pause/terminate API by itself does not establish any of those facts.

## 4. Review decision and remaining owner gate

**`Amendment Proposal Suitable for Owner Review`.** The proposal clearly marks the recommendation as unapproved, identifies controlled termination as a material safety-contract change, preserves the mandatory fail-closed/independent-trust gates, and requires separate versioned baseline effect before source or mechanism work. It does not claim that forceful disposal is currently lawful under the active contract. This review adds the explicit impact on `SIR-02-KM-OE-12-BOUNDED-WORKER-ISOLATION-DISPOSAL-DESIGN-v1.0.md` §3; any future amendment package must resolve that direct prohibition rather than rely on this review as an amendment. Suitability for owner review is **not** amendment approval, design feasibility, operational proof or a reversal of Design STOP.

**Remaining owner decision:** preserve the current no-controlled-termination path, or authorize preparation of the conditional controlled-termination **versioned safety-contract amendment**; defer if the exclusive-boundary and collateral-effect terms cannot yet be stated. No option is selected by this technical review. An owner direction to draft an amendment would not itself effect that amendment or authorize keys, accounts, features, dependencies, configuration, source modification, VM/container execution or WH cases.

## 3. Trust, failure-path and proof assessment

- **Authority / circular trust:** The draft assigns any future force action only to an independently authorized outer owner, distinct from caller, Controller, Worker and watchdog. T1 independent verifier and atomic pre-side-effect attempt claim, P1 independently sealed complete receipt, E1 protected failure ledger and M1 externally authenticated full manifest are retained. The draft does not select or prove those trust roots. Caller/Worker-supplied records, hashes, nonce or frames cannot authenticate them. An outer owner without those independently established inputs must refuse action.
- **Membership / ownership:** Actual expected-versus-observed Node, Worker, identity-helper, conhost where present, and host-managed members must be complete before provider influence and freshly revalidated before every process/resource-affecting call. A PID or nominal VM/container boundary alone is insufficient. Unknown membership, stale/reused identity or an unverified outside effect means STOP; no watchdog, Worker or fallback may acquire termination authority.
- **Failure and evidence:** The draft requires action refusal, native/API failure/timeout, observer failure and exception/`finally` paths to retain immutable external evidence and unresolved disposal, without changing the terminal validation state. A primary audit failure cannot be concealed by E1; an E1 failure prevents candidate PASS. Before an operative successor can be accepted, its WH/SC negative-path matrix must explicitly exercise outer-owner crash or lost response **after** action initiation, partial/native API failure, post-action observer failure, primary audit plus E1 failure, stale boundary identity, unexpected host-managed member, and deadline overrun. The proposal identifies these as fail-closed categories but does not yet supply exact executable tests or observable platform behavior; that is a later source/R&D proof obligation, not current feasibility.
- **Defined bounds and remaining terms:** Existing `T_cancel = 2 s`, `T_dispose = 5 s`, `T_total = 45 s`, and `T_fresh ≤ 500 ms` remain binding. “Approved Worker/provider timeout”, “exclusive boundary”, “outer isolation owner”, and “independent final disposition” are design terms, not validated mechanisms. A future effective successor must identify the triggering timer/event, exclusive admission and owner trust root, exact monotonic start/end, native call completion and independent final-observation event. The proposed helper `≤5,000 ms` is a design target, not direct proof and cannot extend the disposal or total-run bound.
- **Pause and platform capability:** A paused but live boundary is temporary isolation and not exit, disposal or finite completion. A documented VM/container shutdown, pause or termination API is capability information only; it does not establish complete member attribution, no outside effect, actual hang behavior or measured disposition. WH-03/08/09 remain nonproof until separately authorized direct R&D evidence is independently reviewed.

Any audit addendum change is conditional on an approved design changing the evidence schema and would require its own coordinated versioned governance.

The unresolved trust roots, exact timeout trigger, platform mechanism and direct negative evidence are openly deferred to separate design, change control and R&D gates. Thus it is suitable for the owner to decide whether to **retain the current criterion** or **direct preparation of the specified coordinated versioned amendment**. Neither outcome is selected by this review. Suitability does not approve the redline, reverse D2 Design STOP or rejected exact-source preflight, prove disposal, or authorize execution.

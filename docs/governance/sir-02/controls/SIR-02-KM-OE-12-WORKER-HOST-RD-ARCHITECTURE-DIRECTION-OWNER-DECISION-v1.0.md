**Helper bound selected as a design target only:** `T_helper_end_to_end ≤ 5,000 ms`, measured by one monotonic clock from **before identity-helper creation** until independently observed helper exit with all owned handles closed, or a separately approved qualifying non-destructive isolation state. Its deadline is the minimum of that ceiling, the remaining `45,000 ms` total-run budget and the remaining active `5,000 ms` disposal budget; it never resets in exception or `finally` paths. An expired or live/unattributed helper remains **`HelperUnresolved / Disposal Not Proven`**. The selected target is neither measured capability nor permission to run a helper. Startup `8,000 ms`, provider observation `10,000 ms`, closure `2,000 ms`, cancellation grace `2,000 ms`, ownership freshness `500 ms`, disposal/safe isolation `5,000 ms`, and total run `45,000 ms` retain their separate start/end/expiry proof obligations.

## 2. Dependencies, trust separation and unresolved proof

T1 is the independent authority prerequisite for P1 creation/receipt, E1 failure retention and M1 manifest verification. Shared governance may be designed, but **T1, P1, E1 and M1 may not authenticate each other using only caller-supplied material** or a candidate-created receipt, hash, nonce or frame. The owner-selected combination is conditional on a non-circular independent trust root and auditable role separation. P1's full actual member set is a prerequisite to any D2 resource-affecting action; no unidentified member may be exposed to Job-wide or PID-targeted destructive cleanup. M1 must cover any later approved outer entry, receipt producer, isolation component, evidence writer, helper and configuration before those bytes can be considered for execution.

The following obligations remain **unresolved** and require a separately reviewed design, applicable change control, exact source and newly authorized direct evidence:

1. **T1:** identify the independent verifier/key, trust operator, approved decision binding and candidate-inaccessible, atomic, durable, replay-resistant single-use ledger; prove claim consumption precedes the first execution-relevant side effect and survives collisions, replay, audit failure and post-claim crash.
2. **P1:** independently prove creation/assignment and complete actual Node/conhost/helper membership, with `PID + image path + parent PID + creation time + run token + creation/assignment evidence` and applicable retained handle/session/Job identity. Revalidate the full tuple immediately before each process/resource action, including exception, cancellation and `finally`; missing, stale, mismatched or reused identity must STOP before ARM/CIM/action.
3. **D2:** separately define the isolation mechanism, owner, complete member/resource ledger, lifecycle and authority limits; prove finite, non-destructive worker and helper disposal under actual hang, unknown-member and failure paths. `WH-03` and `WH-09` pre-launch STOP and `WH-08` synthetic STOP remain **nonproof**. No `TerminateJobObject`, kill-on-close, `TerminateProcess`, `Stop-Process`, `taskkill`, unknown-PID termination or broader fallback is approved.
4. **E1:** define a protected writer/destination/schema/retention authority independent of primary audit, including what records failure of that path. Mandatory evidence failure must set **`Evidence Incomplete / Candidate Cannot Pass`** while preserving exactly one immutable `Success | Timeout | Fail | Cancelled` terminal state, its Accepted Result Cutoff and state hash. Partial or malformed results must cause zero authoritative mutation.
5. **M1:** independently anchor the reviewed manifest and owner decision, cover every execution-relevant byte and input, verify the actual selected absolute executables/environment, and prove `100%` equality of reviewed and selected runnable bytes immediately before import/spawn. Unreviewed helper, path shadowing or changed configuration must STOP.
6. **Helper target:** retain creation/start, timeout, cooperative signal, observed exit or approved isolation, handle/member state and expiry evidence proving the `≤5,000 ms` target without extending other bounds. Timer expiry, `unref()` and stream destruction are not disposal proof.

If any selected condition proves unavailable or cannot be met safely, **return that item for a new owner decision**. Do not silently substitute T2, P2, D1, E2 or M2, relax a bound or use an unapproved mechanism. Any new key, store, service, dependency, privilege, account/ACL, OS/CIM or repository configuration, architecture or production/runtime change requires its own applicable change control before use; this record grants none.

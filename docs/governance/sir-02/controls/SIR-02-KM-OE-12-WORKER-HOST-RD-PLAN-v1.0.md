## 1. Objective and non-claims

**R&D objective:** determine whether a dedicated non-interactive worker-host boundary can provide **independently attributable worker ownership/provenance** and **bounded, non-destructive worker-boundary disposal** while preserving **Authoritative Validation-Boundary Quiescence**. The R&D must separately measure finite authoritative closure and finite safe worker disposal. It must **not** attempt to prove physical CIM-provider quiescence; a shared provider may continue internally after the validation boundary closes, provided it cannot mutate the authoritative result. No present design or environment is declared feasible by this plan.

The worker-host candidate is materially different from an ordinary child process only if a separately governed supervisor can independently account for the complete host/helper lifecycle, result channel, ownership receipt and safe disposal. Merely changing window style, launching non-interactively, retaining a handle, setting a timeout, or reusing a Job Object does not escape the prior Design STOP. Sandbox/container, service/broker and VM planning are outside this Option 1 authority.

## 5. GAP-A ownership, receipt and pre-action gate

The future receipt must bind **run ID, generation ID, PID, image path, parent PID, creation time, run token, creation/assignment event, independent producer identity, applicable process handle/session/boundary identity, receipt timestamp and probe-independent integrity reference/hash**. The producer must observe and seal the event **before** the worker/probe can influence authoritative acceptance. The worker cannot create, rewrite, reseal or self-attest its complete ownership receipt. Producer and verifier trust must be separate from probe-controlled input.

For each later process/resource-affecting cleanup or disposal action, **Fresh Full Ownership Revalidation = Mandatory immediately before the action**. Re-read the complete tuple, sealed receipt, current process instance and complete applicable helper/member/handle/session set. A PID list, old snapshot or within-window timestamp alone is insufficient. Missing/stale/conflicting identity, PID reuse ambiguity, replay, token mismatch, unexpected member or receipt integrity failure yields **`STOP — Ownership Revalidation Failed`**. No fallback can bypass that stop. This planning contract does not claim GAP-A is satisfied.

## 6. Separate state domains and proposed numeric bounds

Worker lifecycle observations (`Created`, `Ready`, `Running`, `WorkerCompleted`, `WorkerTimedOut`, `WorkerFaulted`, `WorkerUnresponsive`, `DisposalPending`, `Disposed`, `DisposalFailed / Isolated`) do **not** set authoritative validation state. Only the Controller may close to exactly one immutable **`Success | Timeout | Fail | Cancelled`** value under the governed acceptance rules. Closure records run/generation, terminal reason and UTC time, explicit immutable **Accepted Result Cutoff**, accepted count, authoritative version/hash at closure, and revocation. Late/stale results are rejected into separately labeled non-authoritative audit metadata. Final comparison must prove **`authoritative_state_after_closure == authoritative_state_at_closure`**, with accepted post-closure mutation count `0`. Worker/provider exit and disposal remain separate observations.

These are **proposed acceptance thresholds for later technical review**, not measured capability, approved configuration or permission to run. Use a monotonic clock for elapsed bounds and retained UTC timestamps for traceability. A bound expiry fails the relevant case; it never expands cleanup authority.

| Bound | Proposed numeric limit | Start event → end event | Timeout consequence | Rationale for proposed limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `T_start` — worker startup | **8 seconds** | Supervisor's approved creation request → independently sealed receipt plus worker-ready observation | Fail admission; record host/member state and use only reviewed safe cleanup | Limits synthetic startup while allowing receipt/identity capture; actual sufficiency requires review. |
| `T_provider` — provider observation | **10 seconds** | Approved read-only query dispatch → complete observed result or observation deadline | Controller follows timeout path; do not infer CIM-provider completion or worker exit | Bounds the synthetic observation window without asserting physical provider quiescence. |
| `T_close` — authoritative closure | **2 seconds** | Governed normal/failure/timeout/cancellation closure trigger → immutable terminal record, cutoff and result-authority revocation | SC-3 fails; preserve attempted closure and do not report successful validation | Separately limits the trusted state transition even when provider or worker stalls. |
| `T_cancel` — cooperative grace | **2 seconds** | Approved cooperative cancellation request → observed acknowledgement/natural exit or grace expiry | Record unresponsive/pending state; no destructive fallback | Gives a short cooperative opportunity; it is **within**, not added to, `T_dispose`. |
| `T_fresh` — ownership evidence age | **500 milliseconds maximum** | Completion of fresh independent identity/membership observation → immediately following process/resource-affecting call | Re-observe/revalidate or STOP before the call; elapsed age alone never establishes identity | Limits stale-identity exposure while preserving mandatory immediate pre-action revalidation. |
| `T_dispose` — worker-boundary disposal/isolation | **5 seconds** | Post-closure safe cleanup begins (including `T_cancel`) → independently verified `Disposed` or qualifying safe, finite-lifetime isolation | SC-6 fails; retain unresolved owned resources; no unknown-member or destructive cleanup | Distinguishes physical worker/resource outcome from result revocation within a small synthetic bound. |
| `T_total` — one R&D run | **45 seconds** | Run/generation admission start → immutable final evidence seal or fail-closed attempt record | Record bounded-run failure, unresolved state and cleanup evidence; no silent retry | Caps startup, observation, closure, disposal and evidence handling with margin, without masking sub-bound failures. |

An `Isolated` label qualifies for SC-6 only if exact reviewed source and independent evidence prove complete ownership/containment, **finite worker-boundary lifetime**, no result or collateral authority, and a deterministic safe disposal outcome within `T_dispose`. Merely closing IPC, recording `DisposalFailed / Isolated`, leaving an unresponsive host alive without a proven finite lifetime, or observing no PID later does **not** qualify. If this cannot be made safe before the unresponsive-worker case, **do not authorize WH-03**; record `STOP — Safe Hang-Path Disposal Unproven` at source/environment review.

| ID | Mandatory individual PASS criterion for later direct evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **SC-1** | Pre-probe creation/assignment receipt is complete, immutable, independently produced and probe-independent; fresh full tuple matches. |
| **SC-2** | Expected and actual worker/helper/resource membership match completely; no unknown or unattributed member relevant to disposal. |

Future source/environment review and any later R&D run must **STOP / fail closed** on: unknown helper/member; incomplete or stale identity tuple; PID reuse ambiguity; run/generation or creation-event mismatch; unavailable independent provenance; receipt/anchor integrity failure; privilege elevation/UAC requirement; global OS/CIM/service/registry/Group Policy change requirement; dependency or network requirement; destructive or broader-authority fallback; `T_close`/`T_dispose` overrun; post-cutoff authoritative mutation; evidence capture/hash failure; or inability to safely account for a live unresponsive worker. No fallback may bypass a failed ownership, membership, disposal or audit gate.

## 9. Future evidence schema and cleanup/disposal contract

Every case/run must retain: unique run/generation and synthetic tenant/request/correlation identity; exact reviewed and runnable source/config hashes and executable paths; environment/OS/shell/runtime fingerprint; monotonic and UTC start/end/trigger/closure/disposal times; independently sealed creation receipt and producer identity; expected/actual member sets; fresh pre-action tuple/revalidation record; worker lifecycle; provider observation status; one authoritative terminal state/reason; immutable Accepted Result Cutoff and accepted count; result-channel closure/revocation; late/stale arrivals with rejection disposition; authoritative closure/final state hashes and post-closure mutation count; cooperative cancellation; disposal start/end/result and remaining resources; normal/failure/`finally` cleanup order; unexpected member and sanitized native/exception details; independent audit sink/partial-write outcome; artifact manifest with SHA-256 and immutable linkage. Record minimal necessary data, protect tenant identifiers and fields, and **do not log chain-of-thought**.

Cleanup is a first-class safety path. Future reviewed source must use only governed cooperative/natural exit, bounded observation, read-only verification and deterministic disposal of controller-owned handles, channels, temporary files and sessions unless a separately approved safe boundary proves another method. **Fresh full ownership revalidation precedes any process-affecting cleanup.** No PID-only termination, unknown-member termination, Job-wide action on unresolved membership, destructive fallback, authority expansion or unreviewed watchdog path is permitted. Cleanup/finally cannot bypass the primary safety gate. If safe disposal cannot be proven within its bound, retain an immutable unresolved-disposal failure and stop; **cleanup failed safely is preferable to unsafe destructive cleanup**. Do not treat result-channel revocation as worker disposal or claim physical CIM-provider quiescence.

## Evidence and decision rules

- **Static evidence already observed:** committed payload and governance-manifest hashes matched; the machine-manifest SHA-256 was `03d863808a642768151b0b675618bc99fa1e3108304e4f857d92dc6c3c597a33`, and the governance-manifest SHA-256 was `a1a8afe78fd9f4f0499c5456787ff6ef24a150338b594cb28ac4d466e1e8b9f2`. Current `config.json` stops execution. The review observed the exact branches cited below without importing or executing them. No destructive fallback call was found in the committed source.
- **Proposed design:** the minimum changes and acceptance conditions below are requirements for a *future* revised exact-source/config package. Selecting an independent trust anchor, membership mechanism, or bounded safe-isolation mechanism needs separate owner/change-control decisions; this document does not select them for convenience.
- **Behavior still requiring R&D validation:** an independently reviewed replacement source package, exact runnable-byte verification, a separately authorized one-run R&D execution, and independent evidence review would be needed to prove any runtime claim. Static source presence, a pre-launch STOP, or a synthetic branch is not proof of WH success, safe disposal, GAP-A closure, or GAP-B operational closure.
- **Closure rule:** `GAP Closure = Approved Applicable Design/Boundary AND Newly Authorized Direct Evidence`. No proposal, design approval alone, or repeat analysis of the retained package closes a GAP. Future failure evidence must include the triggering condition, exact branch/API, timestamped state transition, ownership check or stated non-applicability, cleanup and resource state, and an independently retained terminal/audit record. Missing evidence is a failed gate, not an inferred PASS.

## WH failure-path completeness and proof boundary

The source's WH branches are *static coverage*, not evidence that they ran or passed. A future R&D case must include an actual negative/failure-path trace as well as any normal trace. WH-01 requires complete result and receipt/ARM ordering; WH-02 a real provider timeout and disposal outcome; WH-04 an injected worker/provider fault; WH-05 a late post-cutoff result rejected without authoritative mutation; WH-06 a stale/wrong-generation result rejected; WH-07 fresh full-tuple mismatch; WH-10 exception/finally, channel and audit failure with complete resource accounting. **WH-03 currently stops before `spawn` and does not prove safe disposal of a hung worker. WH-08 currently exercises a synthetic unexpected-member STOP and does not prove actual complete Node/conhost/helper membership or safe ownership. WH-09 currently stops before launch and does not prove disposal-failure handling.** Replacing those nonproofs requires an approved safe boundary and newly authorized direct evidence; it cannot be inferred from an intended branch or a pre-launch STOP.

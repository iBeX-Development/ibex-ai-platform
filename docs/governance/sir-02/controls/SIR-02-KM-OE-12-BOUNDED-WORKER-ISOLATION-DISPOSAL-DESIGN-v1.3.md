The v1.0 §3 controller paragraph otherwise remains in force, subject only to the explicitly proposed B3 sentence replacement in v1.1: Controller, Worker, helper and watchdog have **no termination authority**. A distinct independently authorized outer owner may consider only the exact reviewed, separately owner-approved action after approved timeout and cooperative grace. It must first verify T1 decision and consumed attempt, P1 independent sealed creation/assignment receipt, complete actual admission, fresh `PID + image path + parent PID + creation time + run token + creation/assignment event` plus applicable handle/session/Job and boundary identity, and exclusive single-run scope. E1 must retain external failure evidence, including primary-audit failure; M1 must independently anchor executable/source/configuration identity. Caller- or Worker-supplied material cannot authenticate itself.

**Deadline and failure rule:** `T_cancel = 2 s` remains inside the non-resetting `T_dispose = 5 s`; `T_fresh ≤ 500 ms` and `T_total = 45 s` remain as in the Worker-Host Plan. The proposed identity-helper `≤5,000 ms` target is **unproven** and cannot extend either deadline. An approved action and independent final-state observation must complete within the original bounds. Unknown or stale ownership, incomplete membership, unverified outside effect, failed native/API or observer result, unavailable external evidence, unbounded live resource, or deadline overrun is **`STOP — Disposition Unproven`**. A paused-but-live boundary is temporary isolation, **not disposal**, Worker/helper exit or finite completion. Exception, timeout, cancellation, provider failure, partial result and `finally` retain separate evidence and cannot widen authority.
